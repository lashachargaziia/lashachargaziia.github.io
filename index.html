Ethnic Favoritism in Authoritarian Courts

Lasha Chargaziia

draft available on request

Exploits a 2019 reform in Russia that randomized the assignment of cases to judges to identify ethnic favoritism in sentencing. I combine a dataset of over two million scraped criminal verdicts with a machine-learning classifier that infers defendant ethnicity from names and study how ethnicity of both defendants and judges shapes conviction and sentencing.
